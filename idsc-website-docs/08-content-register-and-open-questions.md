## 8.2 Open questions to settle

### Design
1. Does Figma contain desktop only, or are mobile/tablet frames also available?
2. What are the approved brand colors and font families?
3. Is the header sticky?
4. Which navigation items have dropdowns, and what are their exact contents?
5. What is the approved destination of Apply Now?

### Content
6. Which program names and Senior High School strands should be displayed?
7. What exact About IDSC, vision, mission, and core values text should be used?
8. Should the IDSC Hymn page show text, audio, video, or a combination?
9. What does the Career page mean in this project?
10. What are the current admission requirements and deadlines?
11. Which academic year does the tuition page cover, and which charges are included?
12. Which alternative payment services are officially supported?
13. What are the current scholarships, privileges, eligibility rules, and inquiry steps?
14. What are the official full names of Crim Lab, HM Lab, and G-Hall?
15. Which announcements and advisories are current and approved for display?

### Implementation
16. Will content be static, stored in a database, or edited through an admin interface?
17. Who can publish and archive announcements?
18. Is a backend required for the assessed project, or can the initial website be informational/static?
19. Are there existing API contracts or repository conventions the implementation must follow?
20. What hosting/domain will be used, and who will maintain the site after submission?

## 8.3 Placeholder removal checklist
Before launch, search the project for these placeholders and replace them with approved content or remove the block:
- `EXAMPLE`
- `(Description)`
- `(Admin Office Image)`
- `(Fee Design Template)`
- `(Template)`
- Repeated example announcement headlines
- Dummy images, fake dates, or unapproved contact details

Do not replace placeholders with invented facts just to make a page look complete.
