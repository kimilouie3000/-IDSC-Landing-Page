# 1. Project Overview

## 1.1 Project name
**Infotech Development System Colleges, Inc. (IDSC) Website**

## 1.2 Purpose
The website is intended to give prospective students, current students, parents, and other visitors one place to find school information. The planning presentation shows a landing page, shared header and footer, an **Apply Now** call to action, and pages covering programs, admission, tuition and payment information, school identity, student organizations, scholarships, facilities, news, announcements, and advisories.

The site should make important information easy to find without requiring visitors to search through unrelated social posts or contact the school for every basic question.

## 1.3 Intended audiences
- **Prospective students:** explore programs, check admission requirements and estimated fees, and find the next step for applying.
- **Parents and guardians:** review admission information, estimated tuition, and available payment or scholarship information.
- **Current students:** find student organization, scholarship, facility, announcement, and advisory information.
- **School staff:** provide accurate information and keep public notices current, if an editing process is included in the final scope.
- **General visitors:** learn about IDSC, its mission and values, facilities, and contact channels.

## 1.4 Project goals
1. Present IDSC and its educational offerings clearly.
2. Make admission-related information accessible from the main navigation.
3. Give announcements and advisories a visible place on the home page.
4. Present facilities, student organizations, and scholarships with appropriate text and images.
5. Keep navigation, branding, and contact information consistent across pages.
6. Work on desktop and mobile screens.
7. Make changing information maintainable.

## 1.6 Outside the confirmed scope
The source material does not establish that the website must include online enrollment, online tuition payment, student accounts, a learning management system, grades, attendance, or a complete admissions application portal. Do not add these as committed features unless the project owner or school approves them.

“Apply Now” is shown as a call-to-action button, but its destination and exact application process are not specified. Confirm whether it should open an external form, show instructions, or lead to an application page.

## 1.7 Project status language
- **Specified:** named or shown in the provided references.
- **Planned:** included in this documentation but not yet confirmed as built.
- **Implemented:** present in the application and checked against acceptance criteria.
- **Needs confirmation:** the reference does not provide enough detail to make a reliable decision.

A mockup is evidence of intended design, not proof that a feature has already been implemented.
