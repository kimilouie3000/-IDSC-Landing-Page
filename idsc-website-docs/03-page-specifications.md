# 3. Page Specifications

This document turns page labels and content blocks in the PowerPoint into a page-by-page implementation brief. It does not invent official school policies or course details. Content needing confirmation must be supplied or approved by IDSC.

## 3.1 Home page
**Purpose:** introduce IDSC and direct visitors to useful information.

**Elements shown in the presentation:**
- School name and tagline
- Main navigation and Apply Now button
- Large hero/feature area
- Featured headline, including the example “Batch 2026, you made it—and you stood out”
- “IDSC Pulse”
- “Announcements and Advisory”
- Announcement/advisory cards with category labels and posted dates
- Footer and contact details

**Requirements:**
- Keep the school identity and Apply Now action prominent.
- Follow the Figma frame for hero composition. Confirm final image, crop, headline, and button placement from Figma.
- Each news card should show a title, category, date, and a link to the full item if detail pages are included.
- Distinguish announcement and advisory items with text labels, not color alone.
- Remove repeated `EXAMPLE` cards and duplicate placeholder headlines before production.
- Show a useful empty state if no current announcements are available.
- Make links to full articles clear and keyboard accessible.

**Content needed:** approved hero content, current featured story, real announcements/advisories, publication dates, and destination URLs.

## 3.2 Programs: College Courses
The presentation lists “College Course” but does not provide an official course catalogue.

Required content:
- Official program/course names
- Short descriptions
- School-approved program images, if available
- Program-specific requirements only if confirmed
- A clear route to admission information

Do not create course names, degree titles, durations, or accreditation claims from assumptions. Ask the school for the current approved list.

## 3.3 Programs: Senior High School
The presentation names Senior High School but does not identify strands. Obtain approved strand/track names, descriptions, eligibility or admission details, and images before publishing.

## 3.4 About IDSC

- Approved images

Do not write a fictional history or make unverified claims about the institution.

## 3.5 Vision, Mission, and Core Values
The presentation lists this content area but does not provide the statements. Use the school's exact approved wording, preserve its headings and order, and confirm whether this is a separate page or a section within About IDSC.

## 3.6 IDSC Hymn

## 3.7 Career
The presentation lists Career under About IDSC but does not define its purpose. Confirm whether this means employment opportunities, career guidance, graduate career information, or another topic before building it.

## 3.8 Admission Requirements
The PowerPoint shows an “Admission Requirement” page with an admin-office image and a `(Description)` placeholder.

Required content:
- Current requirements, grouped by applicant type if lists differ
- Document names and required copies/formats
- Relevant office/contact instructions
- Application periods or deadlines only when officially confirmed
- A route to Apply Now

The actual checklist is absent from the source. Do not publish guessed requirements.

## 3.9 Estimated Tuition Fee
The PowerPoint shows an “Estimated Tuition Fee” page with a fee-design template and a `(Description)` placeholder. The home mockup also contains the example headline “GOOD NEWS: No Tuition Fee Increase at IDSC for AY 2026–2027.”

Requirements:
- Display only school-approved figures.
- State the applicable academic year.
- Explain what the estimate includes and excludes if the school provides that information.
- Identify variations by program, year level, units, or other factors where confirmed.
- Include a note directing visitors to the school for a final assessment when appropriate.
- Show a last-updated date if content is maintained regularly.

The example headline is not proof of a current official fee policy. Confirm its validity before using it as a current claim.

## 3.10 Alternative Payment Service
The page title appears in the PowerPoint, but no providers or process are described.

After confirmation, include available methods/providers, step-by-step instructions, reference or proof-of-payment process, processing time and fees if supplied, and an official support contact. Do not invent payment links, account numbers, transaction fees, or guarantees. Do not collect payment credentials in a basic informational website.

## 3.11 Student Organizations
The mockup shows organization images, descriptions, and an image for all organizations.

Requirements:
- Show the official organization name and approved description.
- Use approved photographs and suitable alt text.
- Confirm whether officers, adviser names, contacts, or membership instructions should be shown.
- Use a consistent card or section pattern.
- Do not invent organizations or list unconfirmed officers.

## 3.12 Student Scholarship and Student Privileges
The presentation lists Student Scholarship, Student Privileges, and “Inquire Scholarship.”

Requirements:
- Explain each school-approved scholarship or privilege.
- State eligibility, required documents, application period, and contact route only when supplied by the school.
- Make clear whether an item is a scholarship, discount, privilege, or inquiry service.
- Keep deadlines and eligibility details easy to update.
- Link to the correct inquiry route.

The presentation does not supply eligibility rules, amounts, or award guarantees. Obtain these from IDSC.

## 3.13 Facilities
The presentation groups facilities into:
- **Laboratories:** CSS Lab, Crim Lab, HM Lab
- **Academic spaces:** Library, G-Hall
- **Specialized spaces:** Clinic, Mock Hotel

For each facility, provide its official name, a short factual description, approved photograph(s), any approved access/use information, and descriptive alt text. Confirm the official full names of “Crim Lab,” “HM Lab,” and “G-Hall.” Do not claim equipment, capacity, or services without confirmation.

## 3.14 News, announcements, and advisories
The presentation shows “Announcements and Advisory,” category labels, dates such as “Posted: May, 2026,” and example headlines.

Requirements:
- Each item should have a title, category, publication date, and body or destination.
- Sort items consistently, preferably newest first.
- Keep announcement/advisory distinctions visible.
- Use a full article view or approved external destination for longer content.
- Do not keep expired information at the top without an archive/status indicator.
- Show a clear empty state rather than fake news cards.

Confirm whether the school needs a content-management interface or will update content through another process.

## 3.15 Apply Now
The button appears in the shared header. Its destination is unspecified. Confirm whether it links to an external form, an existing admissions page, or a future application page. It must not lead to an unrelated page or dead link.
